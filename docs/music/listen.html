I also rendered each part of the band alone and fitted the eleven of them to your clip. That showed my strings 2 dB too quiet against the drums, my hats 3.6 dB too quiet and my bells 1.6 dB too loud, which I corrected. After that, every part I could pin down fits within 1.5 dB.

What I couldn't match, or guessed

  • The sound itself. Everything above is numbers. Whether it has the same feel is for your ears.
  • The tom fill. It is four notes in 34 seconds, too little to measure. It is built to the description and unchecked.
  • The wind. It is too quiet in your clip to measure well. Mine is set by the description and a rough fit.
  • Levels the description leaves out: the click on the kick and the toms, the bell's brighter edge, and how far right the hats sit.
  • How a short string note dies away. I read "exponential release" as fading to nothing within the release time.
  • The ending's levels. Your description gives the ending's notes but not how loud each part is, so those are mine. I also added a knock to the landing and a rattle to the drum roll, because a phone's speaker can't play the low drums.
